01 / Research overview

From rock composition to mechanical response.

The project investigates whether measurable mineralogical and physical properties can be used to estimate two key intact-rock engineering parameters.

01 — CONTEXT

Research Gap

Single-parameter / limited-parameter empirical correlations are mostly used

02 — OBJECTIVE

Aim and objectives

Prediction of UCS, Modulus of Elasticity and cohesion of rocks using their minerology and physical properties.1. Data Collection from Literature 2.Cleaning up of Data 3.Choosing best model and parameters 4.testing of models for untrained data 5.GUI development for easy use and reach to users

03 — OUTPUT

Compare model behaviour

Evaluate tree-based ensemble predictions against explicit sparse polynomial equations using a common held-out test partition.

Central research question

How reliably can intact rock mechanical properties be estimated from mineralogical and physical parameters?

02 / Methodology

Model Overflow

The deployed application accepts six numerical inputs and returns four independent model estimates. We have developed this exclusively for Granite samples.

STEP 01

Dataset preparation

193 complete observations retained for the eight required input and target fields. No records excluded in the documented preparation. ↗ Access research dataset

STEP 02

Model development

XGBoost regressors and degree-two polynomial feature models with LassoCV-selected terms.

STEP 03

Model validation

Common 80:20 train–test partition, with 5-fold cross-validation on the training partition.

STEP 04

Prediction interface

Four trained models are served through a FastAPI endpoint and accessed by this browser-based calculator. Hosted on Render.
